SFS Industries · bodies + lids + bottoms · several sizes on one sheet · all sizes in mm · works offline

1. Job – what goes on the sheets

Choose the mold, change the body height if needed, and tick the parts (e.g. Ø200: body + lid + bottom, Ø175: body + lid only). Lid, bottom and body length are fixed by the mold. Parts with the same size are joined, and the app places everything on the sheets by itself.
Height in the job = can height. Body blank height = can height + curl/flange allowance (e.g. 50 + 8 = 58).

The slitter cuts pieces of the cutting size W × L (e.g. 200×200, or 250×180 for a rectangular can). The press punches the lid/bottom from each piece. Cutting size = lid/bottom size + 2 × margin (5 mm each side). Round with W ≠ L = oval. Leave 0 for a part the mold does not have.

3. Our sheet sizes

The app tries every size, both ways round, and picks the lowest tinplate weight.

4. Gang slitter

Edge margin = scrap at every sheet edge so the blade cuts cleanly (oil cans 5 mm). Pieces narrower than the min knife spacing are not used.
